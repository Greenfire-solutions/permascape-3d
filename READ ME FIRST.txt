PERMASCAPE 3D: READ ME FIRST
============================

Permascape 3D is a permaculture design studio. Type an address, the land loads in 3D with its
real slope, satellite photo, climate and public maps, and you design on it. It runs on your own
Mac, in your web browser. Everything it needs is inside this folder: nothing to install.

You need: a Mac with macOS 12 or newer, a web browser (Chrome, Safari, Edge or Firefox), and an
internet connection, because the land, the satellite photo and the climate are read from free
public map services each time you start a new site.


STARTING IT
-----------

1. Unzip the download and move the "Permascape 3D" folder into your home folder
   (in Finder: Go > Home, the folder with your name) or into Applications.
   Keep everything in it together.

2. Open the folder and RIGHT-CLICK (or Control-click) "Start Permascape", choose Open,
   then press Open again in the box that appears.

   macOS asks this once because the app was not bought through the App Store.

   On macOS 15 (Sequoia) or newer there may be no Open button. Then:
     a. Double-click "Start Permascape" and press Done (or OK) on the warning.
     b. Open System Settings > Privacy & Security, scroll down, and press "Open Anyway"
        next to the line about Start Permascape.
     c. Double-click "Start Permascape" again and press Open Anyway.

3. Your web browser opens Permascape 3D. The very first start can take up to a minute while
   your Mac checks the new files. The first screen says "Opening Permascape 3D" while the 3D
   map loads; on an older or busy Mac that can take up to half a minute.

After the first time, just double-click "Start Permascape". It opens in a few seconds.

If "Start Permascape" does nothing at all, double-click "Start Permascape.command" in the same
folder instead. It does the same thing.


YOUR FIRST TEN MINUTES
----------------------

1. Type an address and press Find. Your own land is best. To look around first, try a public
   farm such as:   Rodale Institute, Kutztown, PA
   Coordinates (40.5520, -75.7466) and Google Maps links work too.

2. Check the square on the satellite preview sits on the land (click to move it), pick a site
   size, and press "Build this site". In under a minute the land is there in 3D, with its real
   slope (1 m lidar for most of the US), climate, frost dates, hardiness zone and soil.

3. Follow the step-by-step card on the right: Read the land (the Analyze tab shows slope,
   water flow and sun), Place the home base, Map the sectors, Water first (swales on contour,
   ponds, tanks), Draw your zones, Plant guilds. Drawing a zone opens a plant guide that judges
   206 plants for that spot and your climate.

4. In the Site tab, press "Load them" under Existing conditions to read the flood, stream,
   wetland, soil and road maps for the land. Then open Review in the top bar: a score out of
   100, with a one-press next step for every weak spot.

5. Open File (top right) > "Report and proposal" for a printable client report with a
   scaled plan, the 3D view, the land, the maps, climate, water and budget.

Permie, the AI designer, can draw a whole first design, critique yours, fill in a client brief
from your notes and write a proposal letter. It needs Claude Code installed and signed in on
this Mac (claude.ai/download, then sign in once). Without it, Permie says so plainly and
everything else in the app works.


GOOD TO KNOW
------------

- Your projects are saved in a folder called "Permascape 3D Data" in your home folder, so you
  can replace the app folder with a new version without losing anything. Back that folder up.
- If one of the public maps does not load (the government map services are sometimes down),
  the review says "Not checked" for it and leaves it out of the score. Press "Try again" in Site
  before you give a report to a client.
- Public maps miss things. Walk the land, and call 811 before anyone digs.


IF SOMETHING GOES WRONG
-----------------------

- A box says the port is in use: another copy of Permascape 3D, or another program, is already
  running. Quit it, then open Start Permascape again.
- The land does not load: check the internet connection and press Find again.
- The details of every start are written to the Logs folder in your Library:
  Finder > Go > Go to Folder... and type  ~/Library/Logs/Permascape3D


MAKE IT YOUR OWN
----------------

All the code is in this folder. Open it in Claude Code (or any code editor) and read CLAUDE.md
first: how it is laid out, how to run it while you change it, and ideas to ask for. If you
change the code, "Start Permascape" notices and rebuilds the app by itself the next time (that
one time needs the internet and a few minutes).
