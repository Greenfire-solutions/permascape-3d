PERMASCAPE 3D: READ ME FIRST
============================

Permascape 3D is a permaculture design studio. Type an address, the land loads in 3D with its
real slope, satellite photo, climate and public maps, and you design on it. It runs on your own
Mac, in your web browser. You do not need to install anything yourself.


STARTING IT THE FIRST TIME
--------------------------

1. Unzip the download and move the "Permascape 3D" folder into your home folder
   (in Finder: Go > Home, the folder with your name). Keep everything in it together.
   It also works from Desktop, Documents or Downloads, but then macOS may ask once
   whether Terminal may open files there: press OK.

2. Open the folder and RIGHT-CLICK (or Control-click) "Start Permascape", choose Open,
   then press Open again in the box that appears.

   macOS asks this once because the app was not bought through the App Store.

   On macOS 15 (Sequoia) or newer there may be no Open button. Then:
     a. Double-click "Start Permascape" and press Done (or OK) on the warning.
     b. Open System Settings > Privacy & Security, scroll down, and press "Open Anyway"
        next to the line about Start Permascape.
     c. Double-click "Start Permascape" again and press Open Anyway.

3. Wait. The first start does two things by itself, and a small notice in the top right corner
   of the screen tells you what it is doing:
     - downloads Node.js, the engine the app runs on (about 50 MB, one to three minutes);
     - sets up one small part it needs (usually under a minute).
   Nothing is installed outside the Permascape 3D folder and no password is asked for.
   You need an internet connection for this first start.

4. Your web browser opens Permascape 3D. The first screen says "Opening Permascape 3D" while
   the 3D map loads; on an older or busy Mac that can take up to half a minute.

After the first time, just double-click "Start Permascape". It opens in a few seconds.

If "Start Permascape" does nothing at all, double-click "Start Permascape.command" in the same
folder instead. It does the same thing in a Terminal window.


USING IT
--------

- Start a project from an address, coordinates or a Google Maps link.
- In the Site tab, press "Load them" under Existing conditions to read the flood, stream, wetland,
  soil and road maps for the land. The design review checks your design against these maps.
- If one of those maps does not load (the government map services are sometimes down), the
  review says "Not checked" for it and leaves it out of the score. Press "Try again" in Site
  before you give a report to a client.
- Public maps miss things. Walk the land, and call 811 before anyone digs.
- Permie, the AI designer, needs Claude Code installed and signed in on this Mac. Everything
  else works without it.

Your projects are saved in the "data" folder inside Permascape 3D. Back that folder up.


IF SOMETHING GOES WRONG
-----------------------

- A box says it could not download Node.js: check the internet connection and open
  Start Permascape again.
- A box says the port is in use: another copy of Permascape 3D, or another program, is already
  running. Quit it, then open Start Permascape again.
- The details of every start are written to the Logs folder in your Library:
  Finder > Go > Go to Folder... and type  ~/Library/Logs/Permascape3D
