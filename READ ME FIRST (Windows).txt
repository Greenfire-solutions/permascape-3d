PERMASCAPE 3D FOR WINDOWS: READ ME FIRST
========================================

Permascape 3D is a permaculture design studio. Type an address, the land loads in 3D with its
real slope, satellite photo, climate and public maps, and you design on it. It runs on your own
computer, in your web browser. Everything it needs is inside this folder: nothing to install.

You need: Windows 10 or 11 (64-bit), a web browser (Edge, Chrome or Firefox), and an internet
connection, because the land, the satellite photo and the climate are read from free public map
services each time you start a new site.


STARTING IT
-----------

1. Right-click the downloaded zip and choose "Extract All...", then Extract. Do not run it from
   inside the zip: Windows only shows the files, it does not unpack them.
   Keep everything in the "Permascape 3D" folder together. Your Documents folder or your user
   folder is a good place for it.

2. Open the folder and double-click "Start Permascape" (Start Permascape.cmd).

   The first time, Windows may show a blue box: "Windows protected your PC". It says this
   because the app was not bought through the Microsoft Store. Press "More info", then
   "Run anyway". Windows asks only once.

   If Windows Firewall asks about Node.js, you can press Cancel: Permascape only talks to your
   own computer and to the public map services.

3. A small black window says "Starting Permascape 3D..." and your web browser opens it. The
   first screen says "Opening Permascape 3D" while the 3D map loads; on an older computer that
   can take up to half a minute. The black window closes by itself after a few seconds, and
   Permascape keeps running.

After the first time, just double-click "Start Permascape". It opens in a few seconds.


YOUR FIRST TEN MINUTES
----------------------

1. Type an address and press Find. Your own land is best. Coordinates (for example
   -37.81, 144.96) and Google Maps links work too.

2. Check the square on the satellite preview sits on the land (click to move it), pick a site
   size, and press "Build this site". In under a minute the land is there in 3D, with its real
   slope, climate, frost dates and an estimated hardiness zone. Outside the US the zone is an
   estimate from climate data: if you know your local zone or frost dates, set them in the
   Site panel.

3. Follow the step-by-step card on the right: Read the land (the Analyze tab shows slope,
   water flow and sun), Place the home base, Map the sectors, Water first (swales on contour,
   ponds, tanks), Draw your zones, Plant guilds. Drawing a zone opens a plant guide that judges
   the plant library for that spot and your climate.

4. Open Review in the top bar: a score out of 100, with a one-press next step for every weak
   spot. On the Brief page, under "You, the designer", set your currency symbol (for example
   €, £ or A$), and set your local prices on the Budget page.

5. Open File (top right) > "Report and proposal" for a printable client report with a
   scaled plan, the 3D view, the land, the maps, climate, water and budget.

Permie, the AI designer, can draw a whole first design, critique yours, fill in a client brief
from your notes and write a proposal letter. It needs Claude Code installed and signed in on
this computer (claude.ai/download, then sign in once). Without it, Permie says so plainly and
everything else in the app works.


GOOD TO KNOW
------------

- Your projects are saved in a folder called "Permascape 3D Data" in your user folder
  (C:\Users\<your name>), so you can replace the app folder with a new version without losing
  anything. Back that folder up.
- Sites outside the United States open in metres and hectares. The US flood, wetland, stream
  and soil maps cover the US only; elsewhere the review says so and leaves them out of the
  score. Check your local government flood and wetland maps before building near water.
- If one of the public maps does not load (map services are sometimes down), the review says
  "Not checked" for it. Press "Try again" in Site before you give a report to a client.
- Public maps miss things. Walk the land, and have buried services located before anyone digs.
- To stop Permascape 3D, restart the computer, or end "Node.js JavaScript Runtime" in
  Task Manager. It uses almost nothing while you are not using it.


IF SOMETHING GOES WRONG
-----------------------

- The black window says the port is in use: another copy of Permascape 3D, or another
  program, is already running. Close it, then start Permascape again.
- The land does not load: check the internet connection and press Find again.
- The details of every start are written to a log folder. Press Windows key + R, type
  %LOCALAPPDATA%\Permascape3D  and press Enter.


MAKE IT YOUR OWN
----------------

All the code is in this folder. Open it in Claude Code (or any code editor) and read CLAUDE.md
first. If you change the code, "Start Permascape" notices and rebuilds the app by itself the
next time (that one time needs the internet and a few minutes).
